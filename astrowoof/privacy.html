Privacy — AstroWoof

AstroWoof is developed by Rudy Spano. This policy applies to the Android and iOS game.

Progress, flight records and language preferences are stored only on your device. The game has no account, chat or in-app purchases. Tilt sensor readings are used locally for steering. No camera, microphone, contacts or precise location are requested.

On Android, Google AdMob may show non-personalised ads between completed levels. Requests are marked child-directed, under the age of consent and limited to G content. The advertising ID permission is removed. The advertising SDK may process IP addresses, device information, ad interactions, diagnostics and identifiers to deliver and secure ads. These requests need Internet. The game itself also works offline.

On iOS, advertising is disabled and the Google advertising SDK is not initialised. The game does not transmit progress, records or language preferences to us.

You can erase local game data by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, or deleting the app on iOS. We do not operate a game-account server. For advertising data, consult Google’s policy.

This game is intended for young children. A parent or guardian can contact us about privacy: rudy.spano@gmail.com
